Stem cells

What is a stem cell?

an unspecialised cell that produces different types of specialised cell/p>

What is the function of stem cells in a young organism?

for growth and production of new specialised cells

What is the function of stem cells in a fully grown organism?

for repair of damaged cells

State one way in which embryonic stem cells are different from adult stem cells.

Embryonic stem cells can produce a wide range of specialised cells, but adult stem cells can produce a much smaller range.
